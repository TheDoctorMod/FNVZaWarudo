ACTIVATION AUDIO OPTIONS

Each installer option selects one complete activation cue:

No Voice:
  Data\Sound\fx\TheWorldNV\active.wav

DIO Brando:
  Data\Sound\fx\TheWorldNV\dio_activation.wav

Jotaro Kujo:
  Data\Sound\fx\TheWorldNV\jotaro_activation.wav

The cues are mutually exclusive and are not layered. The original active.wav is used only by No Voice. The shared resume.wav cue and unrelated gameplay audio are unchanged.

Only redistribute recordings for which you have the necessary permission.
